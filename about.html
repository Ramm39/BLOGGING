About

A small publication for working engineers

BudhiWorks exists because most “ultimate guides” online are either too shallow to ship from or too academic to use under deadline. This site publishes fewer posts — each one meant to answer a real search like “how to fix CORS” or “when to use Postgres indexes.”

Who runs this

Ram — Founder & Editor, BudhiWorks.

I build and ship production web apps — Node, React/Next.js, Postgres, and the boring infrastructure that keeps them online. BudhiWorks is where I publish the guides I wish I had when something broke at 2 a.m.

I am not a faceless “editorial team.” If something is wrong in an article, email me and I will fix it.

Why BudhiWorks exists

“Budhi” points to practical intelligence — judgment you use while building. The site focuses on:

  • Problems people actually type into Google (CORS, JWT cookies, Core Web Vitals, Docker Compose).
  • Guides with copy-pasteable commands and the failure modes I have hit in production.
  • A small catalog (25 strong posts) instead of a flood of lookalike essays.

Editorial standards

Every guide should be usable without ads. We document sources of truth (official docs) on the resources page and follow a public editorial policy.

We do not publish AI-padded philosophy metaphors dressed up as engineering.
