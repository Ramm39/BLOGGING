Policy

Editorial policy

How BudhiWorks decides what to publish, how we update posts, and what we will not do.

What we publish

Guides that answer a concrete developer question with steps you can run. Preference goes to topics with clear search intent (troubleshooting, comparisons, setup checklists).

Accuracy

Commands and configs are checked against current docs when published. If a reader reports a break, we patch the page and note material updates when the change is significant.

Independence

Affiliate or sponsored mentions — if any — will be labeled. Ads never decide article topics.

What we reject

  • Thin rewrites of documentation with no added decision guidance.
  • Mass-produced lookalike posts published on the same day.
  • Philosophy metaphors that do not help you ship or debug.

Corrections

Email contact@budhiworks.com with the URL and what failed. Serious errors get priority fixes.
